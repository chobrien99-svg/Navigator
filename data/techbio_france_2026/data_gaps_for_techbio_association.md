# French TechBio Funding Map: data we could not confirm

French Tech Journal / France Navigator · 10 November 2026 · 48 companies on the October 2026 TechBio list

We built the map from public sources: the company register, press releases, investor announcements, EU, ANR and research-ministry open data, INPI patents and BODACC notices. The points below are where public sources were missing, incomplete or in conflict. Confirmation from the companies, or corrections, would make the map more accurate.

## 2. Public funding

- **France 2030 consortia:** Owkin (RHU AI-TRIOMPH and RHU CONDOR) and Qubit (quantum-computing programme): only consortium totals are published, not each company's share.
- **Other France 2030 and Bpifrance grants:** Aqemia's France 2030 award is recorded; most other companies' Bpifrance or France 2030 grants are not publicly listed.
- **EIC equity:** EIC Fund investments are not public in open data. Recorded: Qubit €5M (2021). Iktos mentions an option for a further €5M with its 2024–25 EIC grant. Ksilink was selected for a 2024 EIC Accelerator, but no amount is recorded.
- **Research tax credit:** 10 companies are approved CIR research bodies. Approval says nothing about the amounts.

## 6. Method limits (for information)

- Founding year is the registration date of the French legal entity, which can be later than when the team started.
- Dollar rounds are converted at the approximate euro rate for the month announced.
- Segment grouping is ours, based on each company's description.
- ANR searches before 2010 rely on name and institution matches, because those records have no researcher IDs.
