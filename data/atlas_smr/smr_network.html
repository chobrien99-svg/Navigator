How this was built

  • Source: the official France 2030 laureates map (info.gouv.fr), filtered on Objectif 1 and exported by hand in November 2026. The map lists 139 rows; one (Innowtech / PACH3) appears twice, so 138 unique rows.
  • Each organisation was matched to the national company register (recherche-entreprises.api.gouv.fr) by name and département. 63 matches are high confidence, 4 are medium and 1 (Entreprise Auglans) could not be found. Medium and unconfirmed matches are drawn with dashed lines.
  • One row (PALLAS) has a project name but no organisation; it is linked to its four named partners only.
  • The map does not give grant amounts, project descriptions or which partner leads. Those are the next things to add.
  • Investors come from funding rounds already in France Navigator, not from France 2030.
