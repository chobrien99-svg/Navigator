Leadership change, 12 November 2026

Jensen Huang stepped down as NVIDIA's chief executive and left its board with immediate effect, according to the company's Form 8-K filing. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and Harvey C. Jones becomes board chair. Huang personally fronted most of the French announcements below, including the October 2026 stakes and his Élysée meeting with Emmanuel Macron. Official record

Open antitrust investigation

France's competition authority raided NVIDIA's Paris office in September 2023 and has been investigating since. In July 2026 it said the investigation was nearing its end. No charges or decision had been announced as of 12 November 2026. Details in section 6. Press, FTJ

Hardware donations and lab collaborations

  • About 295 French papers in HAL thank NVIDIA Corporation for donated GPUs (its academic hardware grant programme), peaking in 2018–2020. A further 54 HAL records name NVIDIA in their funding field. Official record
  • Université de Reims (URCA) was the first French university named an NVIDIA CUDA Research Center. In March 2015 it opened a GPU Computing Application Lab at its ROMEO centre (then France's most powerful NVIDIA Tesla cluster, 260 K20X GPUs); the lab was funded by the region and Reims Métropole, not NVIDIA. University release
  • C2I2 industrial chair in supercomputing and industry at URCA, 2017–2020: NVIDIA was an industrial partner with CEA and Bull (chairholder Michaël Krajecki). NVIDIA's contribution is not disclosed. URCA's CReSTIC lab also lists a research contract with NVIDIA in scanR. University, scanR
  • Maison de la Simulation (CEA/CNRS, Saclay) hosted an NVIDIA CUDA Quantum workshop for the France Hybrid HPC Quantum Initiative in June 2023. Official record
  • Fondation Inria launched a working group on open-source AI adoption on 13 October 2026. NVIDIA is "among the principal contributors and founding members"; Bull, Dassault Systèmes, Hugging Face, Scaleway, Gradium, H Company and others have joined. Findings due spring 2027; NVIDIA's contribution is not stated. The release quotes Nathaniel Ives, NVIDIA's enterprise director for France, Benelux and the Nordics. Fondation Inria
  • Inria / Institut Pasteur: Inria team EPIMETHEE's 2025 report lists NVIDIA under bilateral industry contracts, supporting a project on the foetus, with a memorandum with Institut Pasteur planned. No amount. Inria report
  • Personal gift, not corporate: Franck Diard, an NVIDIA Distinguished Engineer and Polytech Nice Sophia alumnus, gave €400,000 over four years to Fondation Université Côte d'Azur (announced January 2026). It funded a Lenovo supercomputer with NVIDIA GPUs and fab-lab equipment. University

UK–France federated AI (ConcordAI)

Announced 21 October 2026 at GTC Berlin "with technical support from NVIDIA". GENCI and the Bristol Centre for Supercomputing will link Jean Zay and Adastra with Isambard-AI so models can train across both countries without moving sensitive data, using NVIDIA's open-source FLARE tools. The direct link is targeted for the end of 2026. GENCI

Not every French supercomputer is NVIDIA

France's first exascale machine, Alice Recoque (TGCC, installed 2026, open to users in 2027), runs on AMD Instinct MI430X GPUs and AMD and SiPearl processors, integrated by Eviden. Adastra also uses non-NVIDIA hardware. CEA

Researchers' compute centres also list NVIDIA cards in scanR: IDRIS (Jean Zay V100/A100), ROMEO in Reims (280 P100), CALMIP Toulouse (Turpan, ARM + NVIDIA), ICI Nantes (Tesla K80). Paris-Saclay's shared Ruche cluster at IDRIS (CentraleSupélec, ENS Paris-Saclay, Université Paris-Saclay) has more than 80 NVIDIA A100 and V100 cards (CNRS, July 2024). Four small CNRS freight orders (2021–22) mention "DGX" shipments; it is not certain they are NVIDIA systems.

5 · Programmes

Start-up programmes and partnerships

NVIDIA Inception and compute credits

  • NVIDIA says more than 700 French companies are members of Inception, its free start-up programme. Navigator records three: Lucé Robotics, Allude and Percisio (unconfirmed). NVIDIA's own figure
  • AI Factory France (GENCI) × NVIDIA: announced 25 November 2025 at the Adopt AI Summit as Europe's first AI Factory collaboration with NVIDIA Inception and NVIDIA Connect, and NVIDIA joined AI2F's scientific and industrial advisory committee. It went live at VivaTech in June 2026. Eligible members (open research, R&D in Europe) can apply for supercomputer time, including on Jean Zay; it is not unlimited free compute for every start-up. Early users: Pleias, Nebula, Ryax. GENCI
  • Scaleway (November 2023): cloud credits for start-ups on its 1,016-H100 "Nabuchodonosor" cluster, fast-track entry to Inception, and NVIDIA AI Enterprise software on its marketplace. NVIDIA put Inception at more than 4,000 members across Europe. No credit amount is given. NVIDIA blog
  • Sofinnova Partners: DGX Cloud credits for portfolio companies incl. Bioptimus and Cure51 (Jun 2025). Business Wire

6 · Influence and scrutiny

Lobbying, antitrust and government contacts

HATVP lobbying register

  • Brunswick declared lobbying for NVIDIA Corporation in 2020 and 2021: "to present the rationale for NVIDIA's planned acquisition of Arm and its impact in France, in the context of prior authorisation of foreign investment." Targets: MPs and senators and their staff, and members of the government or ministerial cabinets (Economy and Finance). The deal was abandoned in February 2022. Official record
  • NVIDIA Ltd (SIREN 432 108 355) has been listed among the member organisations in AmCham France's register entry since 25 February 2022. AmCham lobbies on its members' behalf. Official record
  • We found no lobbying registration by NVIDIA itself. Spending brackets in Brunswick's filings cover all its clients, so no NVIDIA-specific amount exists.

Antitrust investigation

  • France's Autorité de la concurrence raided NVIDIA's Paris office in late September 2023 as part of an inquiry into the graphics-card and cloud sector. In 2024 its president, Benoît Cœuré, confirmed the probe, telling Reuters NVIDIA would be charged "if the investigation is fruitful". Press, FTJ
  • In July 2026 the regulator said the investigation was "nearing its end". Reported concerns include developers' dependence on CUDA and NVIDIA's investments in AI cloud providers. No statement of objections (the formal charge sheet) or decision had been announced as of 12 November 2026. Press The Next Web
  • FTJ covered the raid (October 2023), the 2024 confirmation and the July 2026 update, but nothing since. The outcome is an open story.

State and Élysée

  • Emmanuel Macron appeared on stage with Jensen Huang and Arthur Mensch at VivaTech on 11 June 2025 and called the Mistral–NVIDIA deal historic. Press
  • Huang met Macron at the Élysée on 13 October 2026, the day NVIDIA announced its French stakes. Press
  • Bpifrance is NVIDIA's co-shareholder in Campus IA and its co-investor in Mistral AI. Press

8 · Gaps

Where we looked and found nothing

  • French public grants to NVIDIA: no ANR project, no research-tax-credit (CIR/CII) approval, no i-Lab or ministry innovation award, no Bpifrance grant listed for any NVIDIA entity.
  • Direct public contracts: NVIDIA is never the contract holder in 3.48 million French public-procurement records (2019–2026).
  • Philanthropy: no NVIDIA Foundation activity or corporate cash donation to a French university found. NVIDIA's documented giving is GPU hardware grants, support for Fondation Inria's working group, and an industrial partnership in the Reims C2I2 chair, all with undisclosed amounts. The €400,000 gift to Université Côte d'Azur came from an NVIDIA engineer personally.
  • Amounts: NVIDIA's own ticket is undisclosed in every French round listed above.
