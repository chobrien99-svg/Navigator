# NVIDIA in France (November 2026)

Search of public datasets for any trace of NVIDIA in the French innovation ecosystem.

- `report/nvidia-france.html`: findings page (published as a private artifact).
- `sources/`: raw extracts
  - company register (`registry.json`) and BODACC notices for NVIDIA Ltd's French branch
  - scanR projects, patents and organisations (gzipped JSONL), and EU projects with NVIDIA participants (`eu_projects.json`)
  - HAL co-authored papers (`hal_coauth.json`) and HAL records with NVIDIA in their funding field (`hal_funding.json`)
  - the 5 genuine NVIDIA lines from the HATVP lobbying actions file (the other 36 matches were "INVIDIA Productions")
  - public-contract (DECP) hits by name and by NVIDIA product name
  - press search results (`press.json`): from search snippets only, the articles were not opened

False matches removed: INVIDIA Concept, Invidia Productions Audiovisuelles, Envidia Systems, Inventures, PhotonVentures.
