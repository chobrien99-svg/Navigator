## Theme: **Top-Heavy**

*A record-looking quarter built on a single deal — and a five-year thinning underneath it.*

**The tension in one breath:** French tech just posted its biggest quarter in a year — €2.09B, up
16% — and it took exactly one deal to get there. Strip out Alan's €480M round and funding actually
*fell*. Underneath the headline, the market has been quietly narrowing for years: fewer deals,
bigger checks, a shrinking base of early-stage rounds. Q2 2026 is that story at its sharpest.

**Why it works as a theme:** it's the same tension the data shows at every altitude — quarter,
year-over-year, six-year Q2 history, and mid-year run-rate all say *bigger numbers, thinner base*.
It also carries forward and deepens Q1's "Great Concentration," giving the two 2026 reports a
through-line.

## The intro (draft prose, ~120 words)

> French tech just had its best quarter in a year. In the second quarter of 2026, startups raised
> **€2.09 billion — up 16% on a year ago**, with four rounds above €100 million. On paper, the
> long-awaited rebound has arrived.
>
> It took one company to make it happen. Alan, the Paris health-insurance unicorn, raised **€480
> million in June** — nearly a quarter of everything raised in the entire quarter. Take that single
> deal out, and French tech funding didn't rise at all. It *fell* about 11%.
>
> That gap — between a record headline and a market that's actually thinning beneath it — is the
> story of Q2 2026. Fewer deals, bigger checks, and a five-year narrowing that this quarter throws
> into sharp relief.

## Narrative arc (the order the report tells its story)

1. **The headline that isn't** — €2.09B / +16%, then immediately the Alan caveat. Set the tension.
2. **The shape of the quarter** — June carried it; April was thin. Sequential slowdown from Q1.
3. **Where it sits in five years** — not a return to the boom; a market in secular decline.
4. **On track to pass 2025 anyway** — the run-rate story, with the outlier asterisk.
5. **The rotation** — AI cools, health and deeptech absorb the capital.
6. **The thinning base** — seed collapse, the structural core of the whole thesis.
7. **Geography & the ecosystem** — Paris re-concentrates; Grenoble emerges; Bpifrance anchors.
8. **Deep-dive** — health/biotech, the quarter's genuine broad-based winner.
9. **Outlook** — what to watch in H2.

## Section-by-section plan

Each section uses the guide's editorial formula: `LABEL → HEADLINE → 1–2 sentence body → chart(s)
→ callout`.

### § The Quarter in One Number
- **Label:** The Headline · **Headline:** *"Two Billion, One Deal"*
- **Body:** €2.09B raised, up 16% YoY — but 23% of it is a single round.
- **Charts:** the 5 headline stats (total, deals, avg, median, mega-rounds).
- **Callout — "The Alan Asterisk":** Alan's €480M is 22.9% of the quarter, the highest single-deal
  share in six years. Ex-Alan, funding fell ~11% YoY. Every number that follows should be read
  twice — with and without it.

### § The Shape of the Quarter
- **Label:** Monthly Rhythm · **Headline:** *"June Did the Heavy Lifting"*
- **Body:** June alone was €1.30B — 62% of the quarter — as Alan, Morpho Labs and Bionyra landed in
  the same month. April was the weak point at €305M, roughly half of April 2025.
- **Charts:** monthly funding (grouped bars, 2026 vs 2025), monthly deal counts.
- **Callout — "The Sequential Read":** Q1 and Q2 posted *identical* deal counts (139 each), yet Q2
  raised 23% less — the difference is one quarter having AMI's €890M round and the other having
  Alan's €480M. Even setting both aside, Q2 was down ~12% on Q1. The median, though, rose.

### § Five Years of Q2s
- **Label:** The Long View · **Headline:** *"The Boom Hasn't Come Back"*
- **Body:** At €2.09B, Q2 2026 is 54% below the Q2 2022 peak (€4.58B). Deal count has fallen every
  year since — 315 down to 139 — while the median check has doubled to a six-year high.
- **Charts:** Q2 total by year 2021–26 (bars); Q2 deal count by year (line, the decline); median
  by year (line, the rise). *These are new charts beyond the guide's original 9.*
- **Callout — "The Recurring Giant":** Every Q2 has one defining round — Contentsquare, EcoVadis,
  Ynsect, Mistral AI, Knave, now Alan. What changed in 2026 isn't that there's a giant; it's how
  much the quarter *leans* on it.

### § On Track to Beat 2025
- **Label:** Mid-Year Run-Rate · **Headline:** *"Halfway to a Comeback — With an Asterisk"*
- **Body:** By June, 2026 had raised €4.83B — already 61% of *all* of 2025, and up 46% on H1 2025.
  On any reasonable run-rate, 2026 clears 2025 and 2024 to become the strongest year since 2022.
- **Charts:** H1 total by year 2021–26 (bars, with a projected full-year 2026 band).
- **Callout — "Flat Underneath":** The rebound is two deals. AMI (€890M) and Alan (€480M) are 28%
  of H1 and about 91% of the entire year-on-year gain. Strip both and H1 2026 is ~4% ahead of
  2025 — essentially flat, on the fewest H1 deals in six years. Bigger headline, same thin base.

### § The Rotation
- **Label:** Sector Intelligence · **Headline:** *"The Money Left AI for Medicine"*
- **Body:** In Q2, AI funding more than halved year-on-year (€513M → €234M) and deal count fell 42%.
  The capital surfaced in health/biotech and deeptech instead.
- **Charts:** sectors by funding (horizontal bars); sector YoY change (diverging bars); sector cards.
- **Callout — "The AI Cross-Check":** Over the full half-year, AI *looks* up 80% — but that's
  entirely AMI's €890M Q1 round. Ex-AMI, AI is down at both the quarter and half-year level. The
  AI wave crested in Q2 2024 (€804M) and has receded two years running. What's genuinely rising,
  with no single deal doing the work, is **HealthTech (+109% over H1)**.

### § The Thinning Base
- **Label:** Stage Structure · **Headline:** *"Fewer Doors In"*
- **Body:** Seed and pre-seed rounds fell from 91 to 57 deals year-on-year — and from a Q2 2022 peak
  of 196 to just 71, a 64% collapse in early-stage deal-making. Meanwhile Series A checks grew 80%.
- **Charts:** stage doughnut (value share); seed/pre-seed deal count by year 2021–26 (line, the
  collapse). *The deal-count line is the single most important chart in the report.*
- **Callout — "The Pipeline Problem":** The funnel that feeds tomorrow's Series A and B is
  contracting fast. Late-stage is near a six-year high (Growth €992M); the base that should refill
  it is at a six-year low. That's what "top-heavy" means structurally, not just rhetorically.

### § Geography & Ecosystem
- **Label:** The Map · **Headline:** *"Paris Reclaims the Center — and Grenoble Arrives"*
- **Body:** Paris took 72% of value (up from 59%), lifted by Alan; regional funding fell 20%. But
  Grenoble stands out — €238M across five deeptech/quantum/medtech deals, second only to Paris.
- **Charts:** Paris vs Regions doughnut; top cities bar.
- **Callout — "The Public Backbone":** Bpifrance touched 22 of 139 deals — more than four times any
  private fund — and is disproportionately present in exactly the early stage that private capital
  is abandoning. The state is increasingly the floor under French seed.

### § Deep-Dive: Health & Biotech (see companion report)
- **Label:** Sector Deep-Dive · **Headline:** *"The Quarter's Real Winner"*
- **Body:** Set Alan aside and health/biotech is the standout genuine story: €480M of core
  health/biotech across 31 deals, led by Bionyra's €140M Series A — one of the largest French
  biotech A-rounds on record — with Sanofi Ventures, Ajax Health and Sofinnova writing big checks.
- Full treatment in `healthtech-funding-report.md` (see guide §9 template).

### § Outlook — What to Watch in H2 2026
- **Label:** Forward Look · **Headline:** *"Can the Base Refill?"*
- Six themes to watch:
  1. **Does the deal count stop falling?** The single metric that decides whether the thinning is
     cyclical or structural.
  2. **Is Alan a one-off or a signal?** Watch whether more €300M+ growth rounds follow — late-stage
     may be genuinely reopening.
  3. **Where does AI settle?** After the AMI/Mistral spikes, find AI's real, ex-mega-deal baseline.
  4. **Health/biotech follow-through** — does Bionyra's A-round mark a durable pipeline or a spike?
  5. **Grenoble** — can the quantum/deeptech cluster produce a second breakout quarter?
  6. **The full-year number** — will 2026 clear €9B, and how much of it is a handful of giants?

## Newsletter article (~850 words) — structure & folded narrative

Voice: conversational, data-forward, occasional aside, ends on tension not a bow (guide §7).

1. **Hook (2 sentences):** Best quarter in a year — €2.09B, up 16%. Then the twist: one deal (Alan,
   €480M) is nearly a quarter of it.
2. **The big number, explained:** Four €100M+ rounds; on paper the rebound arrived.
3. **The outlier caveat:** Ex-Alan, funding *fell* ~11%. This is the whole story in one line.
4. **The mid-year frame (folded in):** Zoom out to the half-year and 2026 is genuinely ahead —
   €4.83B by June, 61% of all of 2025, on pace to be the best year since 2022. *But* two deals
   (AMI, Alan) are ~91% of that gain; strip them and H1 is flat. Bigger headline, thinner base.
5. **Monthly shape:** June carried it (62%); April was weak.
6. **Sector highlights with the AI cross-check (folded in):** The headline that AI "surged" over H1
   is an artifact of one Q1 mega-round (AMI). Look at Q2, or strip AMI, and AI has cooled two years
   running. The real, broad-based winner is health/biotech — Bionyra's €140M A-round leads a genuine
   cluster. Deeptech/quantum (Quobly, Grenoble) is the other riser.
7. **The structural line:** Seed deals down by a third YoY, and 64% off their 2022 peak. The base is
   thinning even as late-stage swells. Bpifrance is now in one of every six deals.
8. **Forward look (2–3 watch-items):** Does deal count stop falling? Is Alan a signal that
   late-stage is reopening? Will the full year clear €9B — and how much of it will be giants?
9. **Kicker (end on tension):** *"French tech will almost certainly raise more in 2026 than it did
   in 2025. Whether that means the market is healthier, or just that a few companies got very big,
   is the question the second half will answer."*
