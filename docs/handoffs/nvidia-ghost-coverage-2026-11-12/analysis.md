## 2. How the story has changed: four phases

### Phase 1 — The GPU gatekeeper (Sept 2023 – 2024)
At first Nvidia mattered to French tech only as **the company that sells the chips**. Coverage focused on who could get H100s:
- Scaleway's DGX SuperPOD (billed as "Europe's most powerful cloud-native AI supercomputer") and OVHcloud's H100 instances (*France Goes Gaga For AI*, Oct 2023).
- Xavier Niel's ai-Pulse, where Huang appeared by video: "getting access to NVIDIA GPUs is now quite often seen as the main constraint for anyone working on GenAI" (*Xavier Niel's Plot To Win AI For France*, Nov 2023).
- Sesterce's H100 supercomputer in Marseille; the government's defence AI supercomputer, where officials admitted that "most of the critical components would come from Nvidia in either case."
- Nvidia appeared now and then as one investor among many in funding round-ups (Hugging Face, Mistral Series B, Poolside).

**From the start, we also covered the tension:** France's competition authority raided Nvidia's Paris office in September 2023. We noted the irony, and later that Huang's warm words for France were "likely also strategic."

### Phase 2 — Nvidia comes to Paris (2025)
**VivaTech 2025 / GTC Paris (June)** was the turning point. It produced our largest cluster of Nvidia coverage:
- *VivaTech 2025: NVIDIA's Jensen Huang On Robots, Quantum Computing, And AI Revolution* — the "Grek" robot, CUDA-Q, "everything that moves will be robotic."
- *VivaTech 2025: Mistral AI Gets Cozy With NVIDIA* — **Mistral Compute**, a sovereign AI cloud built on 18,000 Grace Blackwell superchips.
- Quantum spin-offs: Quandela and Pasqal integrating with CUDA-Q.
- Earlier, at Choose France 2025: the Nvidia / MGX / Bpifrance / Mistral **AI campus** (later described as €35–50bn, in Seine-et-Marne).

In 2025 we also began covering French companies **positioned against Nvidia**. This became one of our most distinctive angles (see section 4).

### Phase 3 — Nvidia as investor (late 2025 – 2026)
Nvidia moved from supplier to **shareholder in French tech**. Investments we reported:

| Company | What it does | What we reported |
|---|---|---|
| Mistral AI | LLMs | Investor in Series B (2024), Series C (2025), €3bn round (2026); co-developing Nemotron 4 base model |
| Scintil Photonics | Silicon photonics (Grenoble) | €50M Series B |
| Alice & Bob | Cat-qubit quantum | NVentures Series B extension (May 2026) |
| Gradium | Voice AI | Seed extension (July 2026) |
| AMI Labs (Yann LeCun) | World models | $1.03bn seed (Mar 2026) |
| Poolside | AI coding ("French-ish") | Reported up-to-$1bn commitment (Nov 2025) |
| Doctolib, Pigment, Genesis AI, Entalpic | Health, finance planning, robotics, materials | Announced by Huang in Paris, Oct 2026 (amounts not disclosed) |

Partnerships and tech integrations we reported: Quandela, Pasqal, Chat3D, Aive (Nemotron), Wandercraft (Calvin humanoid), Pleias (Nvidia Codefest), Weytop, Loft Orbital satellites, Bull (Vera Rubin servers at Angers), LINAGORA/Docaposte Twake.ai, HPE's "AI factory" in Grenoble, Wayve/Stellantis, Microsoft GenAI Studio.

### Phase 4 — Nvidia as acquirer and kingmaker (Aug 2026 – now)
- **Hugging Face acquisition** ($12.93bn, Sept 2026): reported as a rumour (Aug 28), followed through to the closed deal (Sept 4), with a column on whether it counts as a "French" exit (*Is Hugging Face A 'French' Startup? And Should We Give AF?*). This is probably the biggest exit by a French-founded company, and Pollen Robotics comes with it.
- **Huang's Paris reception** (Oct 14, 2026), our most Nvidia-heavy article (17 mentions). Key quotes: "I was here one year ago, and at the time, NVIDIA's investment in Europe was zero… we've invested $8 billion, more than any VC." He also teased another $2bn for European "AI factories," "including France."
- GTC Berlin: Huang playing with Hugging Face Microducks on stage, and the irony that he seemed not to realise Nvidia is buying their maker.

## 3. Recurring themes

1. **Sovereignty versus dependence (the main thread).** We kept pointing out the contradiction: France's "sovereign AI" push runs on American chips. Examples: "Mistral AI's independence is starting to look… expensive" (#69); Mensch: "for now we are relying on Nvidia" (*MistralMania*); "Sovereignty does not mean technological autarky. Mistral still depends on Nvidia chips" (#88); Scaleway CEO on the 2023 GPU shortage: "it was America first, and Europe would come second."
2. **Energy and nuclear.** Nvidia executives keep pointing to French nuclear power as a selling point. Huang: "France has an excellent source of energy." Nvidia's European sales chief told the UK to "be more like France." Nvidia EMEA VP at RAISE: Europe has about 5% of the world's AI compute.
3. **Physical AI and robotics.** From the Grek demo to Wandercraft, UMA, Genesis AI and Pollen/Hugging Face. Huang's argument that Europe "never lost its craft for building things" fits France's robotics story well.
4. **Quantum.** CUDA-Q links Nvidia to all three French quantum leaders (Pasqal, Quandela, Alice & Bob), and Nvidia now holds equity in Alice & Bob.
5. **Open models.** Nemotron Coalition (Mistral, H Company), Aive, Twake.ai, Hugging Face. Huang's line: "Rent when you can, and build if you must."

## 4. Our distinctive angle: the "Nvidia challengers"

This is where our coverage stands out from the competition. We have profiled a whole group of French startups defined by their relationship to Nvidia:
- **VSORA** — *"France's AI David VS Chip Goliath NVIDIA"* (May 2025). This feature then ran as an ICYMI link in about 10 La Machine issues.
- **Arago** (photonic chips) — includes a section called "The NVIDIA Question," where the CEO says Nvidia "can just afford to wait and see who's going to be the winner and just buy them."
- **FlexAI**, **ZML**, **Kog** — hardware-agnostic compute and inference software.
- **Scintil** — a would-be challenger that Nvidia invested in instead.
- **Cerebras** expanding into France (as "the Nvidia challenger").

The Hugging Face deal proved the Arago CEO's point. That could make a strong follow-up: *"Which French startup will Nvidia buy next?"*

## 5. Critical and accountability coverage

We have not been cheerleaders. Critical threads include:
- **Antitrust:** the 2023 raid, the 2024 Reuters confirmation, and July 2026: "France's Nvidia Antitrust Probe Nears Its End."
- **Climate:** James Martin's Responsible AI column says Nvidia's emissions nearly doubled and then "tripled 2024–26," and that 18% of shareholders backed a climate transparency motion.
- **Bubble risk:** idle Blackwell GPUs (Ed Zitron), Nvidia denying an "Enron-like" comparison, and Cathay's Denis Barrier saying Nvidia is "practically full on bookings" for three years.
- **Scepticism about investments:** the Pigment analysis warned that an Nvidia stake "should not automatically be interpreted as evidence of a major commercial partnership."
- **Security:** La Machine #100 covers the alleged Megaspeed chip diversion and espionage story.

## 6. Gaps and opportunities

1. **The antitrust outcome.** Our last update (July 2026) said the probe was "nearing its end." We have published nothing since. This needs checking.
2. **The $2bn European "AI factory" announcement.** Huang said the details would come at GTC Berlin. Our GTC Berlin coverage covered the Microduck moment, not the infrastructure. Which French sites, and how much money?
3. **The Nvidia / MGX / Bpifrance / Mistral AI campus.** Announced at Choose France 2025 and mentioned since only in passing. Is construction under way? Is it on schedule?
4. **Nvidia's own presence in France.** We have never interviewed an Nvidia France executive. The only Nvidia executives quoted are Huang, Bryan Catanzaro (ai-Pulse 2024), David Hogan and Paolo Guglielmini. How many people does Nvidia employ in France, and what happened to the "AI centre of excellence" R&D campus?
5. **A full map of Nvidia's French portfolio.** We now cover about 10 French or French-linked equity stakes plus the Hugging Face acquisition. No single piece brings them together. This suits the Navigator / Funding Hub database well.
6. **Life after the Hugging Face deal.** Does it stay "open, independent and compute-agnostic," as both companies promised? What happens to the Paris team and to Pollen Robotics?

## 7. Data notes and caveats

- Matching was case-insensitive on "nvidia" in titles and full body text (so "NVentures" alone would be missed).
- Many mentions repeat across formats. The same item often runs in La Machine, French Tech Wire, and the Funding Wire. 17 of the 136 posts mention Nvidia only through a repeated sponsor ad (VivaTech/GTC Paris) or a recurring ICYMI link (VSORA).
- Only published posts were included. Pages, drafts and scheduled posts were excluded.
- The full article list (date, title, URL, number of mentions, tags) is in `nvidia_articles.csv` in the same folder.
